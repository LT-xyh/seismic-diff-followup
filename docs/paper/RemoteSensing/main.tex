\begin{abstract}
This work studies seismic velocity-model reconstruction when heterogeneous processed/interpreted constraints are already available. In the controlled synthetic benchmark, these constraints comprise RMS velocity, post-stack migrated imagery, interpreted horizons, and sparse wells. Most learned approaches fuse such observations and assign the complete velocity field to a single predictor or generator. We propose Background-Guided Residual Flow Matching (BG-RFM), which explicitly separates reconstruction responsibilities while retaining multimodal conditioning. A role-aware encoder provides background-oriented and structure-oriented interfaces. The background branch deterministically predicts a smooth velocity component, and the residual branch applies standard conditional Flow Matching to the correction relative to that prediction. Crucially, the same predicted background defines the residual target during training, conditions the residual vector field, and is reused in final composition, yielding a prediction-consistent reconstruction path. On eight OpenFWI-derived subsets with a common 33,600-record test protocol, BG-RFM achieves an RMSE of 0.0269 and an SSIM of 0.9916 with 10.63M trainable parameters. The results support a parameter-compact geophysical reconstruction organization that allocates deterministic estimation to background recovery and generative transport to the prediction-relative correction.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} initial velocity model building; seismic velocity reconstruction; Flow Matching; multimodal geophysical constraints; residual generative modeling; OpenFWI

\section*{Highlights}
\noindent\textbf{What are the main findings?}
\begin{itemize}
    \item BG-RFM combines deterministic background estimation with Flow Matching of the prediction-relative velocity correction.
    \item The model obtains RMSE 0.02695 and SSIM 0.99165 on 33,600 common held-out records with 10.63M trainable inference parameters.
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{What are the implications of the main findings?}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Reusing the same predicted background links residual supervision, conditional transport, and final velocity composition.
    \item Role-aware reconstruction offers a compact generative formulation for integrating processed seismic and geological constraints.
\end{itemize}
