\begin{abstract}
Building an initial seismic velocity model from processed and interpreted geophysical observations requires reconciling information about broad propagation kinematics, reflector geometry, and sparse local velocity control. Although multimodal learning can fuse such observations, the subsequent reconstruction is often assigned to a single full-field estimator. We investigate an alternative organization in which background recovery and the remaining correction have explicit, complementary responsibilities. Background-Guided Residual Flow Matching (BG-RFM) uses role-aware conditioning from RMS velocity, post-stack migrated images, interpreted horizons, and sparse wells. A deterministic network first predicts a smooth background field; a conditional Flow-Matching network then transports the velocity correction relative to that prediction. The same inference-available background defines the residual training target, conditions the residual vector field, and enters final composition. Under a common evaluation of 33,600 held-out records from eight OpenFWI-derived structural subsets, BG-RFM attains an RMSE of 0.0269 and an SSIM of 0.9916 with 10.63M trainable inference parameters. A matched-input benchmark, supporting formulation comparison, and representation/transport diagnostics characterize this reconstruction organization within a controlled synthetic processed-constraint setting. The study offers a parameter-compact approach to coupling deterministic background estimation with prediction-referenced generative correction.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} initial velocity model building; seismic velocity reconstruction; Flow Matching; multimodal geophysical constraints; residual generative modeling; OpenFWI

\section*{Highlights}
\noindent\textbf{What are the main findings?}
\begin{itemize}
    \item BG-RFM combines deterministic background estimation with Flow Matching of the prediction-relative velocity correction.
    \item The model obtains RMSE 0.02695 and SSIM 0.99165 on 33,600 common held-out records with 10.63M trainable inference parameters.
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{What are the implications of the main findings?}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Reusing the same predicted background links residual supervision, conditional transport, and final velocity composition.
    \item Role-aware reconstruction offers a compact generative formulation for integrating processed seismic and geological constraints.
\end{itemize}
