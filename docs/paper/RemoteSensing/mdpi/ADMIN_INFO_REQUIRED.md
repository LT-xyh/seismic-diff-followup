# Administrative Information Required Before Journal Submission

Do not invent these fields. The current MDPI source marks them as administrative placeholders.

1. **Author names** — full publication names.
2. **Author order** — exact final sequence.
3. **Affiliations** — department, institution, city, postal code, country; map each author to affiliation(s).
4. **Corresponding author** — name and email; telephone only if desired/required.
5. **Funding** — exact funder names, grant numbers, and APC-funding wording, or confirmation that the study received no external funding.
6. **Acknowledgments** — non-author technical, administrative, computing, or other support to acknowledge.
7. **Conflicts of interest** — final author-approved statement.
8. **Author contributions** — CRediT roles for each author.
9. **GenAI disclosure** — confirm the exact scope of generative-AI use and approve the final MDPI-compliant wording.

## GenAI policy note

MDPI currently requires disclosure during submission when generative AI was used beyond superficial editing, and requires details of how it was used in Materials and Methods plus product details in Acknowledgments. Superficial grammar/structure/spelling/punctuation/formatting editing is exempt. Because ChatGPT has been used in this project for substantive manuscript drafting/restructuring, the authors should confirm the exact disclosure rather than omit it by default.

Suggested wording for author review (do not use until approved):

- **Materials and Methods:** "Generative AI was used to assist with manuscript drafting, restructuring, and language refinement. It was not used to generate experimental data or replace the authors' scientific validation and interpretation."
- **Acknowledgments:** "During preparation of this manuscript, the authors used ChatGPT (OpenAI; model/version to be confirmed) for manuscript drafting, restructuring, and language refinement. The authors reviewed and edited the output and take full responsibility for the content of the publication."

Also confirm, for the cover letter:
- the manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere;
- all authors approve the submitted version and agree to submission to Remote Sensing;
- whether this manuscript has previously been submitted to any MDPI journal (if yes, provide manuscript ID).
