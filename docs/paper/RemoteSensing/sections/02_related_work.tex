\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Learning-Based Seismic Velocity Model Building}

Deep neural networks have been widely investigated as fast surrogates for seismic velocity inversion and model building. Early fully convolutional approaches demonstrated direct mappings from seismic measurements to subsurface velocity \citep{yang2019deeplearning}, while InversionNet established a strong encoder--decoder benchmark on OpenFWI \citep{wu2020inversionnet,deng2022openfwi}. Subsequent work has explored alternative network topologies, attention mechanisms, transformer-style architectures, and densely connected encoders. Physics-informed and physics-guided methods incorporate additional modeling constraints or wave-propagation structure, while methods such as UPFWI target unsupervised or physics-coupled inversion formulations \citep{jin2022upfwi}. These methods differ substantially in their observation contracts and optimization mechanisms, so source-native literature numbers are not treated as matched comparisons in this study.

\subsection{Generative Modeling for Velocity Reconstruction}

Generative models provide an alternative to purely deterministic regression when the inverse mapping contains fine structural variability or multiple plausible solutions. Adversarial approaches can encourage sharp reconstructions but introduce adversarial optimization requirements. Diffusion and score-based models offer stable distribution-learning mechanisms \citep{ho2020ddpm,song2021scorebased}, and latent diffusion reduces generative computation by operating in a learned representation space \citep{rombach2022ldm}. Flow Matching and rectified-flow formulations instead learn continuous vector fields that transport a simple base distribution toward the target distribution \citep{lipman2023flowmatching,liu2023rectifiedflow}. Recent geophysical studies have applied diffusion-style models to controllable velocity synthesis and inversion \citep{wang2024controllable}. In contrast to full-field generation, \method{} applies generative transport only to the correction relative to a deterministic background estimate.

\subsection{Multi-Source Geophysical Constraints}

Velocity model building can benefit from information beyond raw shot gathers. RMS velocity constrains smooth kinematics and scale \citep{dix1955velocities}; migrated images provide reflector geometry \citep{stolt1978migration}; horizons summarize interpreted stratigraphic interfaces; and well measurements provide sparse local velocity control. Multimodal representation learning offers tools for aligning and fusing heterogeneous observations \citep{baltrusaitis2019multimodal,tian2020cmc,saporta2024symile}. The present work uses multimodal representation learning for a more specific purpose: constructing separate condition interfaces for background estimation and residual reconstruction. The distinction lies in the downstream responsibility assignment, not in claiming that any modality belongs exclusively to one role.
