\section{Discussion}

\paragraph{Why a predicted-background reference matters.}
BG-RFM assigns heterogeneous observations two related but distinct reconstruction roles: deterministic recovery of a smooth velocity background and conditional generative transport of the correction relative to that prediction. The predicted background is an observation-dependent reference shared by residual supervision, vector-field conditioning, and final composition. As Eq.~\eqref{eq:background-centered-path} shows, this is equivalent in full-field coordinates to transporting from a stochastic base centered on the estimated background toward the target. The central contribution is the explicit coupling of these reconstruction responsibilities.

\paragraph{What the evidence establishes.}
The common-protocol comparison shows that Latent U-Net attains the strongest aggregate reconstruction fidelity, while BG-RFM provides a smaller trainable parameter footprint and an explicit background-referenced generative formulation. The retained Flow-Matching formulation study gives supporting descriptive evidence for the complete BG-RFM organization, and the effective-rank, condition-alignment, and target-energy diagnostics characterize the resulting representation and transport reference. Together, these observations establish a structured alternative for the declared task without relying on a claim of overall accuracy dominance or isolated component causality.

\paragraph{Application scope.}
The evaluation reconstructs two-dimensional OpenFWI-derived velocity fields from precomputed synthetic RMS velocity, migrated images, target-derived horizons, and sampled well traces. In particular, the RMS field is integrated from the reference velocity model rather than estimated from noisy field semblance picks, so it provides informative columnwise kinematic constraints. The pronounced performance change under RMS or horizon removal (Appendix~\ref{app:additional}) reflects dependence on this full-input observation contract. Accordingly, the results characterize reconstruction under these controlled processed constraints rather than performance on uncertain field-derived observations.
