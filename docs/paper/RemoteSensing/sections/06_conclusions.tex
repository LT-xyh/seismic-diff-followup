\section{Conclusions}

We presented Background-Guided Residual Flow Matching (BG-RFM) for multi-constraint seismic velocity model building when processed/interpreted constraints are already available. The method learns role-aware conditions from RMS velocity, post-stack migrated images, interpreted horizons, and sparse wells, predicts a deterministic background velocity, and applies standard conditional Flow Matching to the correction relative to that prediction.

The same predicted background is reused for residual supervision, residual conditioning, and final composition. This prediction-consistent organization is not a new mathematical class of Flow Matching; in full-field coordinates it corresponds to transport from a background-centered stochastic base toward the target velocity model. The supporting formulation study is consistent with improved reconstruction for the complete BG-RFM organization relative to simpler full-field Flow-Matching alternatives, but it is not treated as component-isolated or tightly matched evidence.

Under the common 33,600-record comparison, BG-RFM achieves an RMSE of 0.02695 and an SSIM of 0.99165 with 10.6M trainable parameters. The results support a parameter-compact, structured formulation for integrating heterogeneous geophysical constraints without claiming overall benchmark dominance or computational-speed superiority.
