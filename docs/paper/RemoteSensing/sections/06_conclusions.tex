\section{Conclusions}

We introduced Background-Guided Residual Flow Matching (BG-RFM) for seismic velocity reconstruction from heterogeneous processed and interpreted constraints. Role-aware multi-source conditioning informs a deterministic background predictor, while conditional Flow Matching transports the prediction-relative correction. The same inferred background supplies the residual training reference, residual context, and final reconstruction composition.

Across eight OpenFWI-derived subsets and 33,600 common held-out records, the model attains RMSE 0.02695 and SSIM 0.99165 with 10.63M trainable inference parameters. The formulation comparison and diagnostic analyses provide complementary descriptive support for this explicit reconstruction organization. The central result is a compact, prediction-consistent way to allocate deterministic and generative modeling responsibilities: \emph{predict the background; transport the prediction-relative correction}.
