# Remote Sensing Review Checklist

Use this checklist before every major manuscript freeze.

## Story

- Can the paper's problem, gap, insight, and solution be summarized in four sentences?
- Is the main memory point "predict the background; transport the prediction-relative correction" visible early?
- Do the contributions describe what is actually demonstrated?
- Is "physics-decoupled" either precisely defined or removed from headline positioning?

## Evidence

- Does every headline claim map to an accepted evidence artifact?
- Are all main comparison rows evaluated under the same input/split/evaluator contract?
- Are adapted baselines described as adaptations rather than original-protocol reproductions?
- Are parameter-count conventions comparable?
- Are frequency metrics internally consistent?
- Are historical-only numbers clearly separated from new journal evidence?

## Publication-stage discipline

- Does every main-paper experiment strengthen a retained claim?
- Are peripheral diagnostics moved to supplement or removed?
- Does the prose explain advantages explicitly?
- Are non-central metrics reported without turning them into unnecessary competitions?
- Has any local weakness been unnecessarily generalized into a paper-wide negative statement?
- Does the manuscript avoid rebuttal-style prose?

## Scientific integrity

- Are synthetic modality construction procedures disclosed clearly enough for interpretation and reproduction?
- Are target-derived training-only quantities separated from inference inputs?
- Are unsupported field/3D/deployment claims absent?
- Are uncertainty or seed claims supported by the actual protocol?
- Are missing artifacts described internally rather than patched with guessed numbers?

## Figures and tables

- Does Figure 1 show the complete inference logic quickly?
- Is reuse of the predicted background visually explicit?
- Is the main table a matched-input table rather than a cross-protocol literature ranking?
- Do qualitative cases use consistent scales and a fixed selection rule?
- Does each ablation have one interpretable question?

## Abstract

- Important problem?
- Specific gap?
- Distinct method?
- Strongest clean result?
- Practical/scientific significance?
- No debugging history?
- No inflated universal mechanism claim?

## Conclusion

- Repeats the central scientific memory point.
- Uses only claims established in Results.
- Does not introduce new limitations or speculative claims.
